# Internal offer draft — Business Enquiry Assistant Pilot

**Status:** Internal draft only. Not approved for external publication or outreach.

## Customer problem

Small businesses can lose enquiries when responses are delayed, inconsistent, or dependent on one busy person.

## Proposed pilot

A narrowly scoped assistant configured with the business owner's approved FAQs and policies. It prepares response drafts for routine enquiries and flags uncertain or sensitive messages for a person.

## Proposed first package

- One business knowledge set, supplied and approved by the customer.
- One enquiry intake path selected during scoping.
- Grounded draft replies with source references.
- Human escalation for uncertain or sensitive cases.
- A short handover and test report.

## Commercial hypothesis

A one-time CAD $149 setup pilot is a price hypothesis only, adapted from the company's existing Website & Quote-Reply Rescue offer. It is not validated demand, an accepted quote, or revenue. Confirm delivery effort, customer willingness to pay, taxes, and any applicable legal/privacy obligations before offering it.

## Exclusions

- No autonomous sending.
- No CRM migration.
- No guaranteed sales, conversion, or response-time outcomes.
- No payment processing.
- No legal, medical, financial, or other regulated advice.
- No paid services or subscriptions without founder approval.

## Proof required

1. A real business confirms the problem and accepts the scope.
2. The customer approves the knowledge base and sample replies.
3. Tests show drafts cite approved sources and escalate unsupported requests.
4. Founder approves any external offer or outreach.
5. Payment is counted only after a real receipt is independently verified.
6. Delivery is counted only after customer acceptance evidence is recorded.
